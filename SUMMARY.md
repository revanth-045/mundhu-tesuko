Mundhu Tesuko (ముందు తెలుసుకో) — "Know before you apply"

Telangana's Food Security Card portal tells you nothing until it is too late. You cannot check eligibility before applying, so you pay a MeeSeva fee — officially about ₹45, reported charged at ₹250–500 with no receipt — then wait months for a one-line rejection like "income criteria not met". You never learn which rule was applied, whether it was applied correctly, or that the National Food Security Act gives you a right of appeal. Almost nobody knows this one card also gates Aarogyasri, Indiramma housing, fee reimbursement, the ₹500 cylinder and free electricity.

We rebuilt that journey. Seven questions, spoken or tapped, in Telugu or English, give a verdict before you spend anything — with every rule cited, its source named and its confidence shown. Rejections are decoded, including which reasons are commonly applied wrongly, and an appeal letter is drafted for you in Telugu.

Three choices carry the build. Eligibility is computed in plain arithmetic, never by the model; the model only explains the result. Superseded rules are deleted before retrieval reaches the model, so it structurally cannot quote a dead rule — and we show you what was cut. Every claim carries a source label; anything unverified is flagged red, not hidden.

Voice runs in the browser, so it is free and private. Rules, citations and letters need no model at all.

No live government system is touched. All card data is mock. This is not an official service.
